# Zak phase
## Text
One-dimensional Zak phase is defined as [berry_connection.md] integrated over the Brillouin-Zone \cite{ozawa2019topological}

[equation1]

Ir is most commonly encountered in 1D SSH-models, where the it is realised as a pair of topologically protected edge-states.
Due to the $2\pi$ phase ambiguity, Zak phase has two possible values: $0, \pi$.
Thus Zak phase can be identified directly from the symmetry-properties of the mode at $\Gamma$ and $X$-points \cite{vaidya2023topological}

$$\frac{\theta_Z}{2\pi} = \#X_1 - \#\Gamma_1,$$

where $$\#X_1,\#\Gamma_1$$ are the number of even modes at the HSPs over all connected bands.
Essentially, **The system obtains a Zak phase when the symmetry properties of $\Gamma$ and $X$ do not match**.
