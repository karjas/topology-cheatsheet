# Chern number
## Text
[berry_curvature.md] integrated over the Brillouin zone yields the Chern number, a topological charge connected to uni-directional edge bands crossing the band-gap.[ozawa2019topological]

[equation]

Due to symmetry-properties of [berry_curvature.md], if TRS is present, $C_n = 0$.

## Equation
C_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{BZ} d\mathbf{k} \mathcal{B_n}(\mathbf{k})
