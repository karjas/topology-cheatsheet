# Spin Chern number
## Text
Unlike the usual [3,chern_number.md], Spin-chern numbers can exist in systems with Time-reversal symmetry.
They require the total spin in the system to be conserved ($[\hat S_{tot}, \hat H] = 0$), i.e. in which case the spin subspaces are decoupled.

[equation1]

Due to TRS, $C_\uparrow = -C_\downarrow$.
Spin-chern numbers are realised as pair of uni-directional edge states for the different spins, where spin-up edge-states propagate in one direction and spin-down to the other.
