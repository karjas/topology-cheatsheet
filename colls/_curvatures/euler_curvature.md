Non-Abelian Berry curvature is used for calculating the [Euler class]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _global_charges/euler_class.md %}) [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.9.021013)



$$\tilde\mathbf{F}_{n,n+1} = \nabla \times \mathcal{A}_{n,n+1}$$



where $\mathcal{A}_{n,n+1}$ is the [Non-Abelian Berry connection]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _curvatures/euler_connection.md %})



## Connected quantities

| Quantity | connection |
| --- | --- |
| [Euler class]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _global_charges/euler_class.md %}) | $$e_{12} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\oint_{BZ} d\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{12}(\mathbf{k})$$ |
