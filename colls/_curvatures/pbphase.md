Pancharatnam-Berry phase is a geometrical phase [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.91.015006).



$$\gamma = \oint_\mathcal{C}d\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{k})$$



where $\mathcal{A}_n$ is the [Berry Connection]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _curvatures/berry_connection.md %})

The [Handedness weighed sub-charges]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _local_charges/handedness_weighed_sub_charges.md %}) carry a $-\pi$ of PB-phase



$$\gamma = -\pi(q_- + q_+)$$
