Non-Abelian Berry connection is calculated between two adjacent bands $n,n+1$ [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.9.021013)



$$\mathcal{A}_{n,n+1}^{(i)} = \braket{u_n(\mathbf{k})|\frac{\partial}{\partial k_i} u_{n+1}(\mathbf{k})}$$



the curl of which is the [Non-Abelian Berry curvature]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _curvatures/euler_curvature.md %}).
